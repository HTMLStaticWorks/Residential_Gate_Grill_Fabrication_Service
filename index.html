Precision Fabrications Built to Last

We blend high-grade metallurgy with detailed architectural drafting, designing custom gates and safety screens for premium homes, apartments, and commercial projects.

Custom CAD Drafting

Every project starts with a detailed 2D/3D CAD blueprint, ensuring precise fitment to your home's structural columns before we cut any metal.

Laser CNC Cutting

We utilize computerized CNC laser metal cutting to produce intricate modern geometric patterns, floral designs, or custom family crests.

Wind & Load Safety

Gates and high-rise balcony safety grills are engineered to withstand severe wind pressure, secure anchor weights, and accidental impact loads.

Premium Metal Alloys

From architectural-grade aluminum and heavy-gauge mild steel to rust-resistant SS 304/316 alloys, we use certified standard materials only.

On-Site Installation

Our certified installation team handles precise site measurements, transportation, anchor welding, alignment checks, and post-install touchups.
