FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide measurement and installation services?

Yes! We handle the complete process. Our team conducts on-site measurements, transports the custom-fabricated gates or grills to your property, and performs complete anchoring and weld-fitting on-site.

What metal alloys do you support?

We work with high-quality structural materials including heavy Mild Steel (MS) profiles, corrosion-free Stainless Steel (SS 304 and marine-grade SS 316), and lightweight non-corrosive powder-coated Aluminum louvers.

What structural anchors do you use for safety?

We use heavy-duty steel expansion anchor bolts, reinforced column sleeve connectors, and high-penetration structural arc welds to ensure gates and high-rise balcony railings are securely anchored without cracking structural pillars.

Do you offer complimentary site visits?

Yes. If you are located in Chennai, you can book a free site measurement visit. Our drafting engineer will visit your property, verify column dimensions, and present a complimentary 3D design suggestion and pricing proposal.
