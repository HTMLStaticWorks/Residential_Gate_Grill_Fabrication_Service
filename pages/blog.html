Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our gate and safety grill fabrication services. Still have doubts? Drop us a message anytime!

Do you provide measurement and installation at the property?

Yes! We handle the complete process. Our team conducts on-site measurements, transports the custom-fabricated gates or grills to your property, and performs complete anchoring and weld-fitting on-site.

How far in advance should I place a fabrication order?

We recommend booking at least 10–14 days in advance for standard safety grills. For complex motorized driveway gates requiring custom 3D CAD design blueprints, booking 3–4 weeks ahead is ideal.

Can I provide my own theme references or custom CNC gate patterns?

Absolutely! We love working with custom designs. Send us images, CAD drawings, or theme references and our design engineers will incorporate them into your laser-cut panels.

What metal alloys do you support?

We work with high-quality structural materials including heavy Mild Steel (MS) profiles, corrosion-free Stainless Steel (SS 304 and marine-grade SS 316), and lightweight non-corrosive powder-coated Aluminum louvers.

Do you offer smart automated motorized driveway gates?

Yes! We offer smart automation integrations with heavy-duty motorized opening arm kits, remote control keyfobs, battery backups, and safety infrared sensors to prevent slamming.
