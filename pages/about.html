Our Mission

A Legacy of Iron & Steel

Founded in 2011 by Kavya Raghavan, Royal Gates Studio began with a simple mission: to combine high-integrity metallurgy with modern residential architecture. What started as a small local welding workshop has grown into a state-of-the-art metal fabrication facility. We specialize in custom-tailored driveway gates, security window grills, and balcony safety railings, serving residential developments and leading architects across Chennai.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Laser Precision

Every sheet profile and joint alignment is designed and cut using computerized CNC systems for a flawless physical match.

Premium Metallurgy

We select high-grade, certified MS, SS 304, and anodized aluminum profiles, ensuring strength and long-term wind resistance.

Structural Security

All gates, high-rise grills, and railings are stress-calculated and anchored using industrial-grade expansion bolts for absolute family safety.

Bespoke Designs

No two architectural layouts are alike. We design each project from scratch, tailoring pattern geometry, hinges, and lock slots to match your villa theme.
